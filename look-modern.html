About us

What is West Coast Swing?

West Coast Swing is a fun, social dance style suitable for people of all ages and dance backgrounds. It is a great way to get fit, make friends and socialise.

Code 03 Swing is the home of West Coast Swing in Christchurch, and we keep things simple. There's no need to book in for a course: just make it when you can – but we're pretty sure you'll end up addicted. You don't need to bring a partner either. We rotate during our classes, which makes them a great way to meet people and means you get to dance with everyone.

Get in touch! Or just come along any Thursday night to meet our awesome crew and get started dancing West Coast Swing.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before your first Thursday night.

Do I need dance experience?

No! We teach Level 1 (beginner level) every week.

Do I need to book in for a course?

No! Just make it when you can – but we're pretty sure you'll end up addicted.

Do I need to bring a partner?

No! Just bring yourself! We rotate during our classes, which makes them a great way to meet people and means you get to dance with everyone.

Do I need special gear?

No! Just comfy shoes, a water bottle and lots of enthusiasm.

What payment methods do you accept?

We take cash and direct deposits to our bank account (Kiwibank 38-9016-0684081-00). We do not have eftpos transactions at this time.
